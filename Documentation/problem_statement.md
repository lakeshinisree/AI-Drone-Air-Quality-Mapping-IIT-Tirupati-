**Problem Statement :**



**Background :**



Air pollution is a growing concern in urban and developing areas. Pollution from traffic, construction activities, industries, and other sources can negatively affect the environment and quality of life.



**Existing Problem :**



Conventional air-quality monitoring systems mainly depend on fixed air-quality sensors. Although these sensors provide useful measurements, deploying a large number of sensors across an entire region can be expensive and difficult to maintain.



Fixed sensors are also limited in their spatial coverage. As a result, pollution variations across different locations may not be captured effectively.



Pollution-prone areas such as:



\* Traffic congestion zones

\* Construction sites

\* Industrial areas

\* Highly developed urban regions



may remain under-monitored when sensor coverage is limited.



**Need for an Intelligent Solution :**



There is a need for an intelligent, scalable, and cost-effective approach that can provide better spatial understanding of environmental conditions.



Drone-based aerial imagery provides an opportunity to observe large areas from above. By combining aerial imagery with Artificial Intelligence and Computer Vision, environmental features can be extracted and analyzed to identify areas that may be associated with pollution.



&#x20;**Problem to be Addressed :**



The proposed project explores the use of --AI-driven drone imagery and geospatial intelligence--to identify environmental features and map potential pollution-prone regions.



The goal is to transform aerial imagery into useful environmental information that can support better monitoring and data-driven decision-making.
