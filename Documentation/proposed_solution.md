**Proposed Solution :**



**Overview :**



We propose an --AI-Driven Drone-Based System for Real-Time Air Quality Mapping and Pollution Detection--.



The proposed system combines:



\* Drone-based aerial imagery

\* Artificial Intelligence

\* Computer Vision

\* Deep Learning

\* Geospatial Mapping

\* GIS-based visualization



to provide environmental intelligence across large areas.



**How the Proposed Solution Works :**



Drones can capture high-resolution aerial images of selected regions.



The images can then be processed using an AI-based computer vision model to identify important environmental features such as:



\* Roads

\* Buildings

\* Vegetation

\* Water bodies

\* Industrial zones



These features can act as indicators of potential pollution sources.



For example, areas with high traffic activity may indicate vehicle-related emissions, while industrial regions may indicate potential industrial pollution.



&#x20;**Pollution Mapping :**



After extracting environmental features, the proposed system can associate these features with pollution indicators.



The information can then be combined with geospatial data to identify pollution-prone regions and generate pollution heatmaps.



These heatmaps can be displayed through a GIS or interactive dashboard to make the information easier to understand.



&#x20;**Proposed Architecture :**



The overall proposed flow is:



(Drone Imagery → Image Preprocessing → CNN Feature Extraction → Environmental Classification → Pollution Indicators → Geospatial Mapping → Pollution Heatmap → Dashboard / GIS)



&#x20;**Key Benefits :**



The proposed approach aims to provide:



\* Wider spatial environmental monitoring

\* Identification of potential pollution hotspots

\* Reduced dependence on dense sensor infrastructure

\* Scalable environmental intelligence

\* Support for data-driven decision-making
